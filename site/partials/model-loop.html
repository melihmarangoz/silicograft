1. Sheep tibia defect An empty 30 mm defect fixed with a steel plate, loaded with 823 N axially plus about 9.8 N m of bending, distal end clamped.
  2. Cells on a lattice Mesenchymal stem cells, fibroblasts, chondrocytes and osteoblasts sit on a 0.1 mm lattice, where they migrate, proliferate, differentiate and die.
  3. Tissue formed Fibroblasts make fibrous tissue, chondrocytes cartilage and osteoblasts bone. Every other lattice point counts as granulation tissue.
  4. Element stiffness A rule of mixtures over the tissues in each element, averaged over the last 10 days: granulation 0.2, fibrous 2, cartilage 10, bone 17,000 MPa.
  5. Finite-element analysis A linear-elastic CalculiX model of quadratic tetrahedra (C3D10) is solved every 3 days.
  6. Mechanical stimulus Minimum principal strain and hydrostatic pressure (MPa) in each element, averaged over its 4 integration points.
  7. Cell differentiation A Claes and Heigele type rule turns stem cells into osteoblasts, chondrocytes or fibroblasts. This closes the loop back to the cells (step 2).
  8. Healing outcome After 360 simulated days the run gives the tissue map and daily cell and tissue counts.
