# Literature search brief: immediate implant + β-TCP, in silico bone healing

## What I am researching
I am building an in silico mechanobiological model of bone healing around an **immediate dental implant placed in a fresh extraction socket**, with the peri-implant gap ("jumping gap") filled with **pure-phase β-TCP granules**. The model couples an agent-based cell model (MSCs, fibroblasts, chondrocytes, osteoblasts; proliferation, migration, differentiation, apoptosis) with finite-element mechanics. Tissue differentiation follows strain and hydrostatic pressure (Claes & Heigele type rules), and the graft resorbs over time. The base model was published by the Checa group (Perier-Metz et al. 2022, Acta Biomater) and already reproduces their sheep long-bone results.

The study has three stages, and I need papers for each:
1. **Calibration** on a dog extraction socket grafted with β-TCP, without an implant (time course of new bone and residual graft).
2. **Validation** on a dog immediate-implant defect grafted with β-TCP, with and without rhPDGF-BB and seeded bone marrow MSCs (BIC and new bone at 12 weeks).
3. **Prediction** for a human mandibular premolar socket with an immediate implant and Cerasorb M, under a sweep of occlusal load magnitude, for delayed and immediate loading.

The goal is a publishable comparison of model output against experimental and clinical data.

## Papers I want
Quantitative papers: tables or figures with mean ± SD, n and time points. Each category lists what to look for:

**A. Animal histomorphometry (most important).**
- Species: dog (beagle, foxhound, mongrel) or minipig.
- Site: extraction sockets or peri-implant gaps/defects grafted with pure β-TCP (Cerasorb, Cerasorb M, GEM 21S, R.T.R, chronOS, or similar), compared with an empty control.
- Needs at least 2 time points.
- Outcomes: % new bone, % residual graft and % soft tissue/marrow, ideally split by zone (coronal / middle / apical), and BIC when an implant is present.
- Very early time points (days 1-14) are especially valuable.

**B. Human clinical studies of immediate implants with β-TCP in the gap.**
- Design: RCTs or cohorts.
- Outcomes: histology from biopsies, CBCT/CT density (HU), buccal bone thickness or gap fill over time.
- The loading protocol (immediate or delayed) and the implant size must be stated.

**C. Socket geometry.**
- Dog P3/P4 and human mandibular premolar sockets: orifice bucco-lingual and mesio-distal size, depth, and buccal/lingual wall thickness at several levels below the crest.
- Methods: CBCT, micro-CT or histology.

**D. Material and tissue properties.**
- β-TCP granule beds: packing fraction, granule porosity, compressive stiffness of the packed bed, dissolution rate.
- Dog and human alveolar bone: cortical and trabecular elastic modulus.
- Bite forces on premolar implants: magnitude and direction.
- Micromotion thresholds for osseointegration.

**E. Biological rates for growth factors and cells.**
- rhPDGF-BB release or retention from β-TCP over time, in vitro or in vivo.
- Dose-response of PDGF-BB on MSC proliferation, migration and osteogenic differentiation.
- In vivo survival and fate of seeded BMSCs in a scaffold or graft.

**F. Comparable computational models.**
- Mechanobiological, agent-based or FE models of peri-implant healing, socket healing, or bone-graft and scaffold resorption.
- Examples: work by Checa, Geris, Prendergast, Lacroix, Vanegas-Acosta, Chou, or Ambard and Swider.
- Especially models that were compared with dog or human histology.

## Exclude
- Graft other than pure β-TCP (BCP/HA mixtures, xenografts, allografts), unless it appears as a comparator arm next to pure β-TCP.
- Narrative reviews with no numbers. Systematic reviews with pooled numbers are fine.
- In vitro only work for categories A and B.
