Runs. All three ran with the same random seed (12345). The "unbonded plate" does not bond the callus to the plate, as in the model of Perier-Metz et al. (2022, Acta Biomater 145:329). The "bonded plate" is the assumption of our first attempt and produces artificial compression at the plate edge. The "original stimulus" is the authors' code (cartilage at HP < −0.15 MPa), the "Claes-Heigele stimulus" forms cartilage under hydrostatic compression.

Display. In the 3D scene the lattice is reduced to 0.4 mm voxels: when at least 8 of a voxel's 64 lattice points hold one cell type, the voxel takes that type's colour (bone first). The lattice was saved every 10 days, and the days in between are shown as a smooth transition. The volumes in the charts and the table are daily and at full resolution. The green mark is the paper's experimental value at month 3 (about 170 mm³, range 110-230), the orange mark is the same paper's simulation (about 150 mm³). Both were read from the paper's figure.
